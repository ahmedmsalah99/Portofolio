The brief

Built at Hertel Robotics, a robotics startup in Portugal: the autonomy system that lands a multirotor on a moving ship deck in extreme sea and wind, in C++ and ROS 2 on PX4.

C++ROS 2PX4AprilTagVisual servoing

Company work: the code and footage are private.

What I built

  1. State estimation of the drone and the moving deck, fusing AprilTag vision with the ship's GPS.
  2. Guidance: optimisation-based trajectory generation in one phase and visual servoing in another.
  3. A phase-based mission state machine: approach, track, descend, touchdown.

How it works

  1. 1

    Estimate

    Fuse AprilTag detections with the ship’s GPS to track the drone and the moving deck.

  2. 2

    Approach

    Fly to the ship and line up behind the deck.

  3. 3

    Track

    Optimisation-based trajectory generation keeps the drone over the moving deck.

  4. 4

    Descend

    Visual servoing turns the deck’s image position into position and velocity setpoints for PX4.

  5. 5

    Touch down

    A phase-based state machine (approach, track, descend, touchdown) commits to the landing.

Engineering decisions

  • Guidance sends setpoints to PX4 rather than replacing its low-level controllers, keeping the flight stack proven.
  • Two guidance modes, each in the phase where it works best: optimisation far out, visual servoing close in.
  • Validated across 4 simulated extreme sea and wind scenarios, with over 95% successful landings.
